Flooring Installation Services

Professional PVC vinyl and luxury vinyl tile (LVT) flooring installation with precision and expertise.

Installation Process

  1. Surface Preparation: Assessment and leveling with self-leveling compound (up to 2-3mm)
  2. Priming: Application of eco-friendly acrylic primers for optimal adhesion
  3. Laying: Precision cutting and fitting of vinyl flooring with waterproof seams
  4. Sealing: Final sealing for protection and durability

Pricing Guide:

Standard PVC Vinyl: ₹20 - ₹45 / sq. ft.

Heavy-Duty Commercial: ₹45 - ₹80 / sq. ft.

Luxury Vinyl Tiles: ₹55 - ₹100+ / sq. ft.
