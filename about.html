Our Story

Pioneering Interior Excellence for Over a Decade

Founded with a vision to bring world-class interior finishing materials to every Indian home and business, Rana Enterprises has grown from a modest building materials dealership into a comprehensive interior solutions provider with deep expertise in PVC, WPC, and composite materials.

Operating as both a wholesale distributor and a turnkey interior services company, we bridge the gap between wholesale affordability and premium architectural design—serving homeowners, contractors, architects, and commercial developers with equal dedication.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

2014
Rana Enterprises established as a wholesale dealer of standard PVC panels and basic building materials.
2016
Expanded product catalog to include ACP (Aluminium Composite Panels) and UPVC window systems.
2018
Launched full turnkey interior installation services; completed 200+ residential projects in first year.
2020
Added premium WPC Fluted Louvers, UV Marble Sheets, and luxury vinyl flooring to the catalog.
2022
Crossed 750+ projects milestone. Launched dedicated commercial and office interior division.
2024
1000+ projects completed. Recognized as a leading premium interior solutions provider in the region.
